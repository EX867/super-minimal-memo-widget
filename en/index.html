Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the "Super Minimal Memo Widget" app for mobile devices (the "Application"), operated by Artsoft (or Salon de Composition) (the "Service Provider").


1. No Personal Information Collection by the Service Provider

The Application does not use any servers operated by the Service Provider. The Service Provider does not collect, store, share, or process any personal data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. All data created within the Application (such as memos and widget settings) is stored on the user's local device, in a local database built with the Android Room persistence library, and is not transmitted to the Service Provider or to any server operated by the Service Provider.


2. Optional Google Drive Backup

The Application offers an optional feature that lets you back up and restore your memos and settings using your own Google Drive account. This feature is off by default and works only when you choose to use it.

  • Data involved: When you run a backup, your memos and widget settings are uploaded to the Application's hidden, app-specific storage area (the "app data folder") in your own Google Drive. This area does not appear in your regular Google Drive file list and cannot be accessed by other apps. When you run a restore, that backup file is downloaded from this area back to your device.
  • Your consent: The Application accesses Google Drive only after you sign in with your Google account and grant permission through Google's consent screen. You can revoke this permission at any time.
  • Direct transfer: Backup data is sent directly between your device and Google Drive. It never passes through, and is never stored on, any server operated by the Service Provider. The Service Provider cannot view, access, or recover your backup data.
  • Google account information: Sign-in and authorization are handled by Google. The Service Provider does not receive or store your Google account password, and does not collect your Google account information.
  • Purpose limitation: Data accessed through the Google Drive API is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you requested. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose, and it is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Google's own policies: Once your data is stored in Google Drive, it is handled under Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the Service Provider is not responsible for how Google processes it.

3. Third-Party Access & Tracking Technologies
  • No Analytics or Advertising: The Application does not integrate any third-party analytics tools or advertising networks.
  • Google Services: The only third-party service the Application uses is Google's authentication and Google Drive API, and only for the optional backup feature described above. If you never use the backup feature, the Application makes no such connection.
  • No Cookies: The Application does not use cookies, pixels, or any tracking technologies.
  • Apart from the Google Drive backup you initiate yourself, no data is shared with or transferred to any third parties.

4. Data Storage and Deletion
  • Local data: Your memos and settings are stored on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the Application's storage through your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.
  • Google Drive backup: Backup files stored in your Google Drive are not deleted automatically when you uninstall the Application. The Application does not currently provide a function to delete backups. To delete your backup, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings > Manage apps, select this Application, and choose "Delete hidden app data". Because the Service Provider has no access to your backup, it cannot delete it on your behalf.
  • Revoking access: You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the Application from reading or writing your backup, but does not by itself delete the backup file; delete it as described above if you wish.

5. Children's Privacy

The Service Provider does not collect any personal information through the Application, and therefore does not knowingly or unknowingly collect, request, or solicit any data from children under the age of 16 (or any other age). The Google Drive backup feature requires a Google account, and use of that account is governed by Google's own age requirements.


6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy with a new effective date on this page.
